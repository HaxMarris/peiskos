Stockpile

Two to eight players from the fire
How to play
  • Be the first to get rid of every card in your stockpile.
  • The four piles in the middle are shared. Each starts with a 1 and goes up in order to 12, then it's cleared.
  • On your turn, play from your hand, the top of your stockpile, or the top of your discard piles. Wild cards count as any number.
  • Empty your hand during your turn and you draw five more.
  • End your turn by putting one card from your hand on one of your four discard piles.
  • Standard stockpiles are 30 cards, or 20 with five or more players.
  • Tap a card to pick it up, then tap where it goes.
